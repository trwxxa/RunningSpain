St. Peter’s Basilica & The Dome

Our first full day started with a group tour of St. Peter’s Basilica—massive and breathtaking.

We climbed all 551 steps to the top of the Dome. Our calves said otherwise.

Getting lost (in the best way)

Wireless (no overseas phone plan), we used our brains and the Rick Steves guidebook to navigate.

We walked miles for fried pizza and gelato, and ended up wandering near the Roman Forum at dusk—violin music, cobblestone streets, and gratitude.

Colosseum & Roman Forum

Day three: Colosseum, Vestal Virgins at the Roman Forum, lunch and more gelato with new friends.
